# Keyboard Typing Cadence - Signing

I think the cadence of keys, similarly to the stroke of a pen or pencil,
likely reveal a lot about the individual writing. I'd like to study this.

Might reveal current emotions.

Even the speed with which certain key combinations are pressed could give clues as to
a subject's password among other secure and private information.

There are definitely clusters of keys that blend into their own objects,
like atoms in spoken word.

It's some combination of the environment created in the arrangement of keys and how
the hand most comfortably interacts with it. This overlaps with neurological influences;
the available precision of control, and personality differences.

It may be necessary to correlate across phenotypes, maybe on the "kind" of hand.
